FRAUD PATTERN: Unusual Transaction Amount
Transactions with unusually high values compared with a user's normal transaction behavior can indicate potential fraud.

FRAUD PATTERN: Unusual Transaction Timing
Transactions occurring at unusual hours compared with a user's historical behavior may require additional investigation.

FRAUD PATTERN: Unusual Transaction Frequency
A sudden increase in transaction frequency can be a potential indicator of suspicious activity.

FRAUD PATTERN: Unusual Merchant
Transactions involving unfamiliar or previously unseen merchants may require additional verification.

FRAUD INVESTIGATION GUIDANCE
A suspicious transaction should be investigated using multiple signals rather than relying on a single characteristic.

IMPORTANT
The machine learning model provides an anomaly/fraud score. The score should be combined with transaction context and historical information before generating an investigation report.
